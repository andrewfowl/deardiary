Privacy & data

DearDiary has no cloud, no account, and no telemetry. Here is exactly what exists, where it lives, and who can see it.

What is stored, and where

Everything lives in your Windows user profile, under %APPDATA%\DearDiary:

DataLocationLifetime
Raw screenshots (JPEG)screenshots\YYYY-MM-DD\Deleted after 7 days (configurable 1–90)
Timeline, observations, summariesdeardiary.db (SQLite)Forever — it's your journal
Settings & API keyssettings.jsonUntil you change them

What leaves your machine

  • Screenshots are sent to the AI provider you configured, in ~15-minute batches, solely to produce timeline text. With Ollama, they don't leave at all.
  • Timeline text (not images) is sent to the same provider when you generate a summary, standup polish, or ask a chat question.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no crash reporting, no account service. License keys are verified offline.

Consult your provider's data policy for how they handle API traffic (e.g. most offer no-training API tiers).

When recording stops automatically

  • Screen locked
  • Idle for 3+ minutes (configurable)
  • Paused from the tray or the in-app button
  • Trial/license expired (history remains fully accessible)

Sensitive moments

Pause from the tray before password managers, banking, or anything you'd rather not journal. Screenshots wait ~15 minutes before analysis, so you can also delete a specific day's folder from %APPDATA%\DearDiary\screenshots\ before it's processed.

Deleting your data

  • Everything: quit DearDiary from the tray and delete %APPDATA%\DearDiary.
  • Just images: lower the retention setting — cleanup runs hourly.

Backups

Your journal is a single portable file: back up %APPDATA%\DearDiary\deardiary.db and you've backed up every timeline and summary you've ever recorded.
