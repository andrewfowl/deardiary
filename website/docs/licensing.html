Licensing

A 14-day free trial, then one license — bought once or renewed yearly. Keys activate offline, and your history is never locked behind payment.

The trial

Starts on first launch. Full features, no account, no card. The sidebar shows days remaining during the final week. When it ends, recording and analysis pause — everything you recorded stays fully browsable.

Buying & activating

  1. Purchase on the pricing page. Checkout is handled by Stripe.
  2. Right after checkout you land on a page showing your license key (DDRY-…). It's also retrievable any time with your checkout receipt.
  3. In DearDiary: Settings → License, paste the key, click Activate.

Activation is offline — the key is a cryptographically signed statement of your purchase, verified locally. No license server needs to be reachable for the app to run.

Annual renewals

Each annual key is valid for 366 days. When your subscription renews, a fresh key is issued — check your renewal receipt, or write to support. The app shows a renewal reminder during the last two weeks.

Moving to a new PC

Install DearDiary and paste the same key. Licenses cover two of your own machines. Your journal itself can be moved by copying %APPDATA%\DearDiary\deardiary.db.

Lost keys & refunds

  • Lost key: email support@deardiary.app from your purchase address.
  • Refunds: 14 days, no questions asked.

For self-hosters

The app is MIT-licensed and the entire licensing stack is in the repo (server/ — a small Cloudflare Worker + Stripe webhooks, and scripts/issue-license.mjs for manual issuance). Selling it as your own product requires generating your own signing keypair; see server/README.md.
