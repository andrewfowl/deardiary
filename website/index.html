How it works

No timers. No tagging. No willpower required.

Install it once, and every day writes itself.

1

It watches quietly

A screenshot every 30 seconds while you're active. It pauses when you step away or lock your screen, and lives in the system tray with one-click pause.

2

AI writes the timeline

Every ~15 minutes, the AI provider you choose reads the batch and writes what you were actually doing — projects, documents, sites — categorized and de-duplicated.

3

You cash it in

Daily journal entries, instant standup updates, focus stats, and a chat that answers questions about your own time. Your memory, outsourced.

Privacy

Your screen is sensitive.
We built like it.

A tool that watches your screen has to earn trust. DearDiary's answer is simple: your data never belongs to us, because we never have it.

  • 100% local storage. Screenshots, timeline, and summaries live only on your PC — there is no DearDiary cloud.
  • Nothing recorded without consent. Recording starts only after you finish first-run setup, and pauses from the tray any time.
  • Screenshots self-destruct. Raw images are deleted after 7 days (configurable). The written journal is yours forever.
  • Bring your own AI. Frames go only to the provider you configure — or to a local model via Ollama, so nothing leaves your machine at all.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things everyone asks before letting an app watch their screen.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere, by default. Screenshots and your journal database are stored in your Windows user profile. The only network traffic is to the AI provider you configure — and if that's Ollama running locally, there is none at all.

Do I need to pay for an AI provider too?

Usually not. Google Gemini's free tier comfortably covers typical daily usage, and Ollama is free. If you prefer Claude or OpenAI, you bring your own API key and pay them directly (typically cents per day).

What happens when my trial or annual license ends?

Recording and analysis pause — that's all. Every day you already recorded stays fully browsable, summarizable history. We don't hold your own data hostage.

Can I pause it for sensitive work?

Yes — one click on the tray icon. Capture also stops automatically when your screen locks or you go idle.

Lifetime vs annual — what's the difference?

Features are identical. Lifetime is a single $49 purchase that never expires. Annual is $19/year and keeps working while the subscription is active. Both cover two of your own PCs.

Is there a Mac version?

DearDiary is Windows-first. On macOS, check out Dayflow, the open-source app that inspired us.
