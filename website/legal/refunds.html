Refund Policy

Fourteen days, no questions asked. And a free 14-day trial before that, so ideally you never need this page. Last updated: July 2026.

Lifetime purchases

Full refund within 14 days of purchase — just email us from your purchase address. The license key is deactivated for future issuance; your recorded data stays on your machine and remains yours.

Annual subscriptions

  • Full refund within 14 days of the first payment.
  • Renewals: we'll refund a renewal charge if you ask within 14 days of it — sometimes the reminder email gets missed; that shouldn't cost you a year.
  • Cancelling stops future charges; the license keeps working until the end of the paid period.

How to request one

Email support@deardiary.app from the address you purchased with (or include your Stripe receipt). Refunds are issued to the original payment method, typically appearing within 5–10 business days.
