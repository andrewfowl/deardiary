Privacy Policy

The policy is short because the data flow is short: we designed DearDiary so we never hold your data. Last updated: July 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing, on our side. Screenshots, your timeline, journal entries, settings, and API keys are stored only on your computer (%APPDATA%\DearDiary). There is no DearDiary account, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no crash reporting. License keys are verified on your machine without contacting us.

What leaves your machine

  • To your AI provider: screenshots (for timeline analysis) and journal text (for summaries, standups, chat) go to the provider you configure — Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, or a compatible endpoint — under your own API key and their privacy terms. With Ollama running locally, nothing leaves at all.
  • Nothing to us. The app makes no network requests to DearDiary services.

When you buy a license

Checkout is handled by Stripe, which processes your payment details — we never see card numbers. We receive and store the email address associated with the purchase, solely to issue your license key, handle support, and process refunds. We don't send marketing email.

This website

The site is static and contains no analytics or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard, short-lived request logs (IP address, user agent) as part of operating the service.

Deleting your data

  • App data: it's on your machine — quit DearDiary and delete %APPDATA%\DearDiary. Done.
  • Purchase records: email support@deardiary.app and we'll delete your license record (note this revokes lost-key recovery; keep your key).
