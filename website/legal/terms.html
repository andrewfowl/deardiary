The software

DearDiary is a Windows application that records your screen locally and uses an AI provider you configure to generate a private work journal. The application's source code is available under the MIT license; these terms cover the paid license service — license keys, updates, and support.

Trial and licenses

  • The 14-day trial is free and full-featured. When it ends, recording pauses; everything you recorded stays accessible.
  • A Lifetime license is a one-time purchase valid indefinitely, including all future updates.
  • An Annual license is a yearly subscription; recording works while it is active, and your recorded history remains yours if it lapses.
  • Each license covers up to two computers you personally use. Don't share or resell keys.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe; we never see your card details. Prices are shown at checkout and may change for future purchases, never retroactively. Refunds: see the refund policy — the summary is 14 days, no questions asked.

Your data and your AI provider

Your recordings and journal are stored on your machine and are yours. When you configure a cloud AI provider, screenshots and journal text are sent to that provider under your own API key and their terms — choose a local model (Ollama) if you don't want that. See the privacy policy.

Fair warnings

  • The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. We work hard on reliability, but AI-generated journal content can be wrong — don't treat it as a legal record of your time.
  • You're responsible for using DearDiary lawfully — e.g. not recording screens, accounts, or material you're not permitted to record, and complying with your employer's policies.
  • We may revoke keys that are resold, shared publicly, or obtained fraudulently.
