Your diary starts here.

A full-featured 14-day trial. No account, no card, and nothing is recorded until you press Start.

First run, in three steps

  1. Run the installer

    DearDiary opens with a short setup wizard explaining exactly what it records and where.

  2. Pick an AI provider

    A free Gemini key is the fastest start. Ollama keeps everything offline.

  3. Press Start recording

    Your first timeline appears within about 15 minutes. Then go back to work.

If Windows SmartScreen appears: new releases can show "Windows protected your PC" until the build earns reputation. Click More info → Run anyway, and compare the SHA-256 checksum against the file you downloaded if you want certainty.

Good to know

  • Everything DearDiary records stays on your PC. See privacy & data.
  • After the trial, it's $49 once or $19 a year. Your history stays readable either way.
  • Stuck? The troubleshooting guide covers the common first-day questions.
